# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vietnamese households and people interested in clean food. They visit to learn about the Bao Tieu Xanh brand, browse the produce catalogue, and try the demonstration ordering and order-tracking flow.

## Product Purpose

Introduce agricultural products through a clear, trustworthy storefront and let visitors explore a sample purchase journey. The shop, order tracking, and administration areas are demonstration features; product details and prices are illustrative.

## Brand Personality

Modern, natural, trustworthy, refined, and professional. The interface should help visitors feel confident and make product information easy to understand.

## Anti-references

- Avoid generic, template-like presentation and arbitrary typography changes.
- Avoid effects or interface elements added only for decoration.
- Do not introduce colors that conflict with the existing green-led brand palette.

## Design Principles

- Make Vietnamese content easy to read, including all diacritics.
- Keep product and order information clear and consistent across the storefront and demo tools.
- Preserve the existing brand colors and meaningful content.
- Make the experience readable and usable across phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use complete Vietnamese glyph coverage, body text at least 16px, comfortable line lengths, and responsive layouts. Do not disable browser zoom. No additional accessibility standard or accommodation was specified.
